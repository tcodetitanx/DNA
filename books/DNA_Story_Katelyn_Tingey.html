Serotonin System

Mood regulation, emotional processing, and anxiety

The serotonin system is often called the "mood neurotransmitter" system, though its functions extend far beyond simple happiness. Your serotonin system genetics shape your baseline emotional tone, your sensitivity to stress, and how you process both positive and negative experiences.
