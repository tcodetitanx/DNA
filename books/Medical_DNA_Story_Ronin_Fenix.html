Clotting

Blood clotting and thrombosis risk

Blood clotting disorders (thrombophilias) increase the risk of dangerous blood clots in veins (DVT, pulmonary embolism) and sometimes arteries. These conditions are particularly important to know about before: - Surgery or prolonged immobility - Pregnancy or estrogen-based contraception - Long flights or car trips

Carriers of clotting variants should inform all healthcare providers and may need prophylactic anticoagulation in certain situations.
